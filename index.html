Crystal

Fabricated installation | MAT | 2026 | Jess Yi

These are the crystals I 3D Printed for Media Arts & Design. We displayed our installation, "Crystal Consciousness". A cave that "With the measurement of your human pulse, beats with responding optic fiber lights" Additionally, EEG data was transferred into audio signals as a modulating source within the virtual eurorack and a projection of the body of a nerve cell that moved and changed color with the brain data. With the use of Blender modeling and 3D printing, we hung transparently colored crystals from all around as well as glass pieces acting as sound and light transmitters. My role was in fabrication, 3D modeling and 3D printing.

Scissor

Photography & Storytelling | 2026 | Scissor

Designed and fabricated, hand-sewn bonnet on model. A candlelit poetry portrait series exploring intimacy, innocence, and power. Shot in a space lit only by flame, the images focus on a female subject uniting ancient divinity in the eternal earth with a softness that almost misleads you. The warm, low light reflecting in her eye, bewitching a shadow across the collarbone. The series was made by three women.
