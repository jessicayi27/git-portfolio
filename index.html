Crystal

Fabricated installation | MAT | 2026 | Jess Yi

Crystals I 3D Printed for our installation, "Crystal Consciousness". A cave that "With the measurement of your human pulse, beats with responding optic fiber lights. Moving crystals change color based on your heartbeat. While they process EEG data into audio signals. Transparent crystals hung from all around as well as glass pieces acting as sound and light transmitters. My role was in fabrication, 3D modeling and 3D printing.

Scissor

Photography & Storytelling | 2026 | Scissor

Designed and fabricated, hand-sewn bonnet on model. A candlelit poetry portrait series exploring intimacy, innocence, and power. Shot in a space lit only by flame, the images focus on a female subject uniting ancient divinity in the eternal earth with a softness that almost misleads you. The warm, low light reflecting in her eye, bewitching a shadow across the collarbone. The series was made by three women.
