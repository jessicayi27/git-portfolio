Crystal

Fabricated installation | 2026 | MAT | Jessica Yi

These are the crystals for our final Media Arts & Design project, "Crystal Consciousness". This installation was designd as a cave that "With the measurement of your human pulse,​ beats with responding optic fiber lights" Additionally, EEG data was transferred into audio signals as a modulating source within the virtual eurorack and a projection of the body of a nerve cell that moved and changed color with the brain data. With the use of Blender modeling and 3d printing, we hung transparently colored crystals from all around as well as glass pieces acting as light and sound transitioners. My role was in fabrication, storytelling, 3d modeling and 3d printing.

Scissor

Photography & Storytelling | Scissor | Jessica Yi

Designed and fabricated, hand-sewn bonnet on model. A candlelit poetry portrait series exploring intimacy, innocence, and power. Shot in a space lit only by flame, the images focus on a female subject uniting ancient divinty in the eternal earth with a softnes that almost misleads you. The warm, low light reflecting in her eye, bewtiching a shadow across the collarbone. The series was made by three women.
